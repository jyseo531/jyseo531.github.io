Perceive · motion

Ego-Bi: telling apart actions that differ only in direction

IEEE Access 2026, first author

Tightening and loosening a screw look the same in a single frame. Existing datasets barely contained such bidirectional pairs, so models confused them.

  • Recorded real hand-tool work from the first-person view: 1,200+ sequences, 622,737 frames, 19 fine-grained actions, and wrote the annotation guide.
  • Designed the Bidirectional Motion-Prior (BMP) module, which accumulates rotation and wrist direction from 3D hand joints over time.
+8.96%pTop-1 accuracy on Ego-Bi, 83.15% → 92.11%, with gains on FPHA and H2O as well

Reason · knowledge

M³KG-RAG: retrieval that checks its own evidence

CVPR 2026, second author

Audio-visual LLMs given outside knowledge often get worse answers, because single-hop graphs and embedding search pull in facts unrelated to the clip.

  • Built a pipeline in which seven role-specific LLM agents turn raw audio, video and text into a multi-hop knowledge graph with no human labeling.
  • Proposed the core idea of GRASP: keep a retrieved fact only if visual and audio grounding confirm it appears in the clip, then let a small LLM prune what does not help the answer.
  • Set up the LLM-as-a-Judge protocol and hand-scored samples to check the judge.
+9.23LLM-as-a-Judge points over prior work, with win rates up to 86.3% across audio, video and audio-visual QA
Paper
Predict · 3D worlds

EditSplat: text edits that stay consistent from every view

CVPR 2025, third author

Editing a 3D Gaussian Splatting scene with a 2D diffusion model view by view makes the result disagree between viewpoints.

  • The method fuses multi-view information into diffusion guidance and trims Gaussians where attention says the edit must happen.
  • My part: reproduced the baselines, ran the comparisons, selected and checked qualitative results, and built the project page.

Predict · physics

Recovering what objects are made of, from video

In progress, first author, submission planned for Nov 2026

A world model should know not only how a scene looks but how it reacts when touched. This project estimates hidden material properties from multi-view video of dynamic scenes.

  • Redefined contact between different materials in a differentiable form, after the standard contact handling made training unstable.
  • There are no ground-truth labels, so I built the evaluation from simple cases whose physical quantities are known.
